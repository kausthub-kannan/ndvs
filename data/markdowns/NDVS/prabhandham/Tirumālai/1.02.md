## english_scripts

paccai mā malai pōl mēni
pavaḻa vāy kamala cceṅgaṇ

āyar tam kozundē ! ennum

## meaning

Oh Lord Acyuta possessing a huge, emarald mountain like body, rosy lip of precious corals, and reddish lotus-like eyes! "Oh Lord of the Nityasuris of the Paramapadham! O dear young lad of the cowherds!" thus do I like to chant forever. If so I don't have the chance to chant your holy name at Vaikunta, let that mokasha be never given. How will I ever leave the elixir taste of chanting "Ranga!" at Srirangam and go there? Oh Lord of Thiruvarangam!

## purport

Toṇḍaraḍippoḍi Āḻvār sings the glories of the beautiful thirumeni of Bhagavān. He narrates by personifying the thirumukham of Bhagavān. His lips are smooth like pearls and corals. The color of his lips resembles that of a rose. His eyes, which can attract any jīvatma (and hence are red by their nature of attraction), look like lotus petals. His thirumeni is as huge as mountains and shines like emeralds. He is the Lord of Nityasuris, who rules Paramapadham. If the Lord decides to grant Ālvār the capacity to rule Vaikuṇṭham itself, but if there he could not sing about Ranga, Ālvār says, "What is the use of such liberation if I cannot enjoy Your glorious names? After tasting the elixir called Nāma Saṅkīrtanam here in Śrīraṅgam, I deny such liberation! Oh Lord of Śrīraṅgam!"
