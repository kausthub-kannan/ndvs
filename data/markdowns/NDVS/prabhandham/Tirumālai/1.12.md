## english_scripts

namanum muṟkalanum pēśa
naragil ninṟārgaḻ kēṭka
naragamē śuvarkkam āgum
nāmaṅgaḻ uḍaiya nambi
avanadūr araṅgam ennādu
ayarttu vīzndaḻiya māndar
kavalaiyuḻ paḍuginṟār enṟu
adanukkē kavalginṟēnē !

## meaning

Raṅganātha, whose name you can ever chant and be rid of miseries! Oh supreme Lord with all the auspicious qualities and endowed with numerous divine names! When Yama and sage Mudgala were conversing with each other (i.e., when Mudgala referred to Your divine names), and when those residing in the hell (the abode of Yama) heard Your divine names, the hell was, indeed, fortunate to turn itself into heaven (because of the greatness of Your names). When there are so many divine names of Yours, I am very much worried about the people who fall down out of miseries and are afflicted by sorrow. Because of ignorance, they do not know Your divine grace and the efficacy of your divine names and the divine abode Śrīraṅgam.

## purport

The remembrance of Bhagavān can remove any sins and grant divine liberation itself. It can indeed even convert the depths of hell into the great heavens itself. Āḷvār proves this point by remembering the incident which took place in hell when Mudgala was taken there. When Yama and Ṛṣi Mudgala are having a conversation about the dynamics which the jīvātmā suffers when it is subjected in hell and the pleasure in heaven, the great seer speaks about how these are temporary and chants the name of Nārāyaṇa which would grant Paramapada itself. On chanting the nāma of Nārāyaṇa once, hell for a second turned into heaven. The jīvātmās who with a Sūkṣma Śarīra were subjected to pain according to their Karmas, were liberated from such torture and pious birth was given to them. Such are the glories of chanting the divine names of Bhagavān which would burn Karmas of those who even listen to it. 

How great are the glories of the Lord's names, which can burn the sins of the greatest sinners. Our Lord possesses infinite divine qualities and hence infinite divine names. Āḷvār is astonished by the fact that even after having such an easy route to burn down our Karma, i.e., by chanting his divine names, even after knowing that he possesses not just one or two but countless such names, how foolish are people who are engaged in Saṃsāric activities forgetting about the Divine. Due to their ignorance and arrogance, they are destined to fall and be stuck in this cycle of saṃsāra. How unfortunate are these who do not realize the glories of Your divine grace and Your divine abode, Śrīraṅgam.
