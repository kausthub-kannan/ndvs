## meaning

Oh Lord Ranganatha residing at Srirangam! O supreme Lord with God- dess Laksmi and the Śrivatsa mark adorning your broad chest! all the people on this earth should ever contemplate upon You and your consort, Goddess Sridevi, and then, they should install only both of You in their heart. If they do so, then even if they have committed the most heinous crimes, and so acquired such sins, they will not suffer from the fruits of those sins.

## purport

Hearken, gentle souls, to the wisdom of the Alvar, who once more doth proclaim the ineffable nature of the Divine Lord. He standeth beyond the confines of prakriti's elements, His essence a mystery that mortal minds can scarce comprehend. Verily, His virtues and qualities be infinite, stretching far beyond the reach of our humble understanding.

The Alvar, in his profound insight, declareth that only the Lord Himself knoweth the full measure of His divine play, His wondrous Leelas that unfold across the tapestry of creation. The sacred Taittiriya Upanishad, in its venerable verses, doth proclaim "aprāpya manasā saha" - even as the mind striveth to grasp His nature, it returneth baffled, unable to fathom the depths of His glory.

Yet, take heart, for though He be beyond the reach of common thought, He deigns to reside within the sanctum of pure hearts. As the hallowed Vishnu Sahasranama doth attest, He is "yōgihṛddhyānagamyaṃ" - accessible through the meditations of yogis whose minds are unburdened by impure thoughts. Thus, the Lord maketh His abode in the hearts of His true devotees.
The Alvar, in his boundless wisdom, counseleth us to acknowledge our limitations. How can we, mere mortals, hope to sing the praises of He whom even Brahma and Rudra, mighty deities themselves, find beyond their capacity to laud? Nay, our mortal tongues and finite minds fall short in this endeavor.

Therefore, the Alvar guideth us toward a path most true and efficacious. Let us, in our devotion, turn to the chanting of His divine names and the constant remembrance of His glory. For in this sacred practice lies the key to accessing His divine presence. What sweeter pursuit can there be, what nobler endeavor, than to invoke the Supreme Lord through the utterance of His holy appellations?

Indeed, the Alvar assureth us, there existeth no higher or more potent means to draw near to the Divine than through the sincere and devoted chanting of His names. In this simple yet profound practice, we find the essence of bhakti, the purest form of devotion that bridgeth the gulf between the mortal and the Divine.
