## english_scripts

maṟam śuvar madiḻ eḍuttu
maṟumaikkē veṟumai pūṇḍu
puṟam śuvar ōṭṭai māḍam
puraḻum pōdaṟiya māṭṭīr
aṟam śuvar āgi ninṟa
araṅganārkkāṭ ceyyādē
puṟam śuvar kkōlañjeydu
puḻ kauva kkiḍakkinṟīrē !

## meaning

O idiotic men! You do not know when you are to die, for you are building the fort of adharma (māyā) and you ever think of your perishable body as a cover (of protection); you are increasing your poverty by sowing the seeds of rebirth. It is a pity that you don't take to the divine service under the great Lord of Śrīraṅgam, the supreme Wall of Dharma. Therefore, you are just engrossed in nourishing (and taking care of) your outer wall of śarīra which the vultures would eventually tear into pieces.

## purport

Continuing from the previous pāsuram, Āḻvār continues to question the deeds of the idiotic men who are behind saṃsāric pleasures. Unable to realize the perishable character of this body, their mind is covered by māyā. They keep performing actions which pleasure the indriyas. While doing so, they forget to realize they can be adharmic. Addicted to that temporary pleasure, they keep performing the same actions again and again, leading them to create a huge pitfall of adharma itself! They might be rich materialistically, but alas! They are poor in reality as rebirth is assured to such fools. Eventually, he loses everything, even his body perishes. What is the use of nourishing that body with all care and no efforts taken to use that body to serve the Īśvara, the Lord of Śrīraṅgam who is the protector of Dharma himself! Such fools perish stuck in the cycle of saṃsāra!
