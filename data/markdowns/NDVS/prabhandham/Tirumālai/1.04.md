## english_scripts

moytta val vinaiyuḻ ninṟu
mūnṟezuttuḍaiya pērāl
kattira pandum anṟē
parāṅgadi kaṇḍu koṇḍān
ittanai aḍiyar ānārkku
iraṅgum nam araṅganāya
pittanai ppeṭrum andō !
piṟaviyuḻ piṇaṅgumāṟē !

## meaning

Kṣtrabandhu, son of Viśvavrata, performed such vikarmas which resulted in him accumulating tremendous sins. Just by uttering the name "Govinda," he was released from all sins. Alas! You are here as the supreme Master Raṅganātha, who showers His grace just by looking at the devotee. I am indeed surprised that when You are here to grant the abode itself to the devoted chanters, why are people still ignorant and indulged in saṃsāric activities?

## purport

The Govinda nāma is considered very auspicious and precious due to the beautiful meaning behind it. Govinda refers to the protector of Earth (a reference to the Varāha avatāra). It also refers to Him being the protector of cows as Gopāla. It infers that He is the protector of the ones who surrender to Him. Hence He is also praised as prapanna pārijāta. Āḻvār specifically refers to this name here relating to the goal of liberation from the cycle of saṃsāra. Once one surrenders to the lotus feet of His, that jīvātmā has Vaikuṇṭha assured to him. The Lord in the form of Varāha promised it as "ahaṃ smarāmi madbhaktaṃ nayāmi paramāṃ gatim," i.e., "Those who remember me when they are healthy, I will grant them the supreme abode." Again as Kṛṣṇa, He stated "mām ekaṁ śaraṇaṁ vraja ahaṁ tvāṁ sarva-pāpebhyo mokṣayiṣyāmi mā śucaḥ," i.e., "Abandon all other means to attain mokṣa, just surrender to me. I myself will be the means and take care of removing all your sins and grant salvation to you. Do not fear!" Hence, the nāma Govinda infers these meanings of the specific avatāras. The protector of Bhū Devī, the protector of cows, will indeed protect us from this saṃsāra! 

Āḻvār is surprised now! Even after the Supreme established such an easy means to attain Him, just by chanting His divinity, alas! Why are people still bound by māyā, running after temporary materialistic things?
