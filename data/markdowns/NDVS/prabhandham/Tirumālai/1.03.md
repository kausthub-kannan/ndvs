## english_scripts

vēda nūl pirāyam nūṟu
maniśar tām puguvarēlum
pādiyum uṟaṅgi ppōgum
ninṟadil padinai āṇḍu
pēdai pālagan adāgum
piṇi paśi mūppu ttunbam
ādalāl piṟavi vēṇḍēn
araṅga mā nagaruḻānē !

## meaning

Oh Lord who recides in Srirangam, the Vedas and Shastras are many and are huge. Life span of lofty humans like us doesn't cross 100. In those years, we spend half of it by sleeping. The other half is spent in childhood, youth, old age filled with misery. Oh Ranga! In this run of life this birth leaches out most of the time leaving very little to perform Kainkaryam to you. Hence, I ask you Lord of Thiruvarangam, I do not want to be born again.

## purport

The Vedas and Śāstras prescribe multiple ways known as Brahma Vidyā, which often require the jīvatma to perform upāsana. But these Śāstras are vast in number, with thousands of texts. The human lifespan itself falls short of 100 years (and gets shorter). How is it possible to explore the Vedas with such limited time in which we spend most of it sleeping and the other half indulging in temporary pleasure and pain? What is the use of such a birth where one cannot perform service to the divine Lord? No doubt, after spending such a life, the jīvatma is reborn in this saṃsāra. 

Hence, Ālvār pleads to Ranganātha not to grant him rebirth. If such a birth denies performing service to the Lord, what is the use of it? Therefore, Ālvār requests Ranganātha to grant Paramapadham. (In the previous pāsuram, Ālvār stated that if in such Paramapadham there is no bliss of chanting the names of the Lord, he denies it. But in reality, the Taittirīya Upaniṣad states that the Nityas and Mukhtas keep singing the glories of the Lord. Hence, in this pāsuram, Ālvār requests to grant such a liberation.)
