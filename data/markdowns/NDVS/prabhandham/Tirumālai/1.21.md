## english_scripts

paṇivināl manamadonṟi
pavaḻa vāy araṅganārkku
tuṇivināl vāza māṭṭā
tollai neñjē ! nī śollāy

aruvarai anaiya kōyil
maṇi anār kiḍanda āṭrai
manattināl ninaikkalāmē

## meaning

Oh my adamant heart, hearken! please spell out the words of reply. Owing to your haughtiness you are wasting your life-time. Through utter modesty, you can attune your mind with the Lord Raṅganātha of coral lips. You can also submit your mind always to think of the effulgent reclining posture of the precious emerald- gem-like Lord Raṅganātha dwelling in the great massive Temple, which is an enchanting embodiment of the golden Meru mountain, as it were; why don't you do so?

## purport

Alvar speaks to his mind to rememebr the great Lord. He communicates to his tounge to chant the divine names of Bhagavan. Instead of wasting time on Samsric thoughts think about the Leelas of Bhahavan. The latter provides bliss (Anandam) unlike the temproory happiness provided by the former. Thinking about the divine form of the Ranganatha who posses the coral like lips increases ones Sattvica Guna. When Sattvica Guna increases, he devotes himself more towards Bhagavan. Hence Alvar calls out the mind to think about the divine form of the Lord. Let the mind think about Rangantha who is reclining on Adishesha. Let the mind thing about Srirangam which is covered by walls which are the personification of the divine mountain Meru.
