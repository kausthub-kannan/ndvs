## meaning

Oh my destitute mind! answer me. Even the scriptures abound in the adorations of Him (Lord Raṅganātha), but they cannot fully encompass his supreme qualities. He cannot be known by the parviscient persons, because only men free from impurities can know Him. We should merely worship Him and be content with it; for, He rests only in the heart of persons with purity of mind. Is there anything we can say more about Him?

## purport

Alvar quotes again that how he is beyond the prakriti tattvas and hence no one can know him completly. Indeed his qualities and infinte and apar from our capability understand. Only he knows the Leelas he perform. The Taiterrya states aprāpya manasā saha .i.e even when the mind tried to reach him and understand Bhagavan, it returned baffled. But he resides in the heart of Yogis and devotees who have their mind free from impure thoughts just like Vishnu Sahasranama quotes it as yōgihṛddhyānagamyaṃ.

Hence, Alvar quotes that our mind is not capable of praising him when even Brahma, Rudra and other devatas could not praise the Supreme Lord. Hence, the best way for us to access him to chant his divine names and remember him. How else can we access him easily? Indeed there is nothing better than chainting his divine names.
