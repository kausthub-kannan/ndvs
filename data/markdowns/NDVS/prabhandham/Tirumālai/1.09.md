## english_scripts

maṭrum ōr teyvam uṇḍē

uṭra pōdanṟi nīṅgaḻ
oruvan enṟuṇara māṭṭīr

## meaning

Oh people incapable of reasoning! Is there any God other than Śrīman Nārāyaṇa? None at all. You cannot know Him (Śriyaḥ-pati), because you do not recognize Him as the supreme Master, owing to your immature logic. Whatever is the most Supreme, i.e., beyond your ken, you are not ready to understand. Know Him, Lord Nārāyaṇa alone as God. There is no God other than Him. May you resort to Him (Lord Raṅganātha), nay, to His unexcelled pair of feet, the Lord who, as Śrī Kṛṣṇa, tended the hosts of grazing cows.

## purport

Āḷvār continues to speak to us that without a doubt the one who has Śrī as his divine consort, the one who houses her on his chest is the Supreme Lord indeed! In the previous pāsuram, Āḷvār proves the Parattva of the Lord in his Śrī Rāma avatāra; here he does the same with Śrī Kṛṣṇa as Gopāla. He calls those who cannot understand the fact that Śrīman Nārāyaṇa is supreme as people who cannot reason; their minds have rotted and hence are unable to think critically and logically. Indeed these people are fools! These immature-minded fools think that the cowherd Kṛṣṇa is a mere human. To such personalities, Śrī Kṛṣṇa himself quotes in Bhagavad Gītā: "avajānanti māṁ mūḍhā mānuṣīṁ tanum āśritam," i.e., "Fools deride Me when I descend in the human form." Āḷvār questions those: what can make you fools understand that Nārāyaṇa is the Supreme Lord? Indeed there is no one second to him. He alone is the Īśvara and not any other devatā. To those who have realized it, surrender to his lotus feet! Surrender to him who tends and protects the grazing cows (again Āḷvār refers to Bhagavān as Govinda, the protector of cows as well as Śaraṇāgatas).
