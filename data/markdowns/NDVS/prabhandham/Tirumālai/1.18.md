## english_scripts

ini tirai ttivalai mōda
eṟiyum taṇ paravai mīdē
tani kiḍandaraśu śeyyum
tāmarai kkaṇṇan emmān
kani irundanaiya śevvāy
kaṇṇanai kkaṇḍa kaṇgaḻ
pani arumbu tirumālō
en śeygēn pāvi yēnē !

## meaning

Oh Lord, who reclines on Ādiśeṣa in Śrīraṅgam, I am unable to withstand the beauty of Your divine form. Those rosy lips resembling bimba fruits and the red lotus-like eyes are mesmerizing my mind. You have reclined on the divine ocean whose waters are cool and soothing. What a sinner am I that my tears obstruct the view of Your divine form!

## purport

Continuing from the previous pāsuram, Āḻvār narrates the exquisite beauty of Bhagavān's divine form. Similar to the second pāsuram of Tirumalai, Āḻvār expresses his unbearable love for Bhagavān's divine form. That divya maṅgala rūpa possesses soft, rosy lips and eyes resembling lotus petals (kamala-nayana). Indeed, His form is mesmerizing, and whoever beholds it is stunned. Our minds can scarcely imagine a spark of such glory. 

Witnessing such a divine form, it is impossible for the devoted Āḻvār to hold back his tears. As they flow from his eyes, they hinder Āḻvār's view of the divine Lord. Āḻvār laments what a sinner he must be, unable to fully enjoy the Lord's divine form! Bhagavān's divya maṅgala rūpa, once seen, makes any devotee yearn to gaze upon it again and again. Unable to view His divine form is separation from Him, and thus Āḻvār cries out that his tears obstruct his view.
