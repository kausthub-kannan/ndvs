## tamil_scripts

நாவினால் நவிற்றுஇன்பம் எய்தினேன்மேவினேன்அவன் பொன் அடி மெய்ம்மையேதேவு மற்றறியேன்குருகூர் நம்பிபாவின் இன் இசைபாடித் திரிவனே

## english_scripts

nāvināl naviṭru
inbam eydinēn
mēvinēn
avan pon aḍi meymmaiyē
tēvu maṭraṟiyēn
kurugūr nambi
pāvin in iśai
pāḍi ttirivanē

## meaning

My tongue overflowed with bliss as I recited Āḻvār’s pāsurams. I have completely surrendered at his divine feet. I know no refuge apart from this Āḻvār of Thirukkuṟugūr, adorned with all auspicious qualities. With his pāsurams set to music, I shall go everywhere, joyfully singing his glories.

## purport

Madhurakavi Āḻvār continues to sing the glories of Nammāḻvār with unbounded devotion. Such is the greatness of his Ācārya that even without understanding the deeper meanings behind Nammāḻvār's sacred names, merely reciting them fills the heart with an indescribable sweetness. What divine taste lies hidden within those names! Their nectar is not dependent upon intellectual comprehension; it is experienced directly by the devotee whose heart has become attached to the Ācārya.

The feet of Nammāḻvār have completely captivated Madhurakavi Āḻvār. With pure, unwavering, and exclusive devotion, he has surrendered himself entirely unto them. So profound is his attachment that he declares that he knows no other deity apart from Nammāḻvār. This is the highest expression of *Ācārya-bhakti*. For Madhurakavi Āḻvār, Nammāḻvār is the one who revealed Bhagavān, instructed him in the truths of the scriptures, and transformed his life. Therefore, his devotion remains firmly fixed upon his Ācārya.

He marvels at the boundless compassion of Nammāḻvār. The mercy of the Ācārya is immediate and tangible, reaching out to uplift even the most fallen jīvātmā. It is this extraordinary compassion that overwhelms Madhurakavi Āḻvār and causes him to celebrate his master in the strongest possible terms. Through Nammāḻvār's grace, he has gained access to the sweetness of Bhagavān and the path leading to eternal service.

Overcome with emotion, he praises the divine pāsurams of Tiruvāymoḻi. To him, these hymns are not merely compositions but an inexhaustible ocean of nectar. Bhagavān, Nammāḻvār, the Tiruvāymoḻi itself, and the musical recitation of its verses merge into a single experience of divine bliss. Every word of Tiruvāymoḻi is saturated with devotion, every verse reveals profound truths, and every melody melts the heart of the listener.

The sweetness of these pāsurams surpasses all other forms of delight. Their musical recitation appears to Madhurakavi Āḻvār even more melodious and nectar-like than the chanting of the Sāma Veda itself. The ears never tire of hearing them, and the heart never becomes satiated with their beauty. The more they are recited, the greater becomes the longing to hear them again.

Therefore, Madhurakavi Āḻvār resolves that he will sing and recite these sacred hymns everywhere. He desires to spend his life celebrating the glory of Nammāḻvār and spreading the nectar of Tiruvāymoḻi throughout the world. His sole refuge, his greatest treasure, and his ultimate goal remain the sacred feet of Teṉkurugūr Nambi.

Thus, he declares his complete surrender to the beneficent Nammāḻvār. Having tasted the sweetness of his Ācārya's names, teachings, and divine hymns, he seeks nothing else. The feet of Nammāḻvār alone are his shelter
