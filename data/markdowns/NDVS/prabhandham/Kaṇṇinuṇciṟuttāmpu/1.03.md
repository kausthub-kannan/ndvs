## meaning

I, who was once the devoted servitor of Āḻvār, lost hold of that noble position. Yet by his grace, I beheld Śrīman Nārāyaṇa, the dark-hued sovereign of the eternal nityasūrīs. Such wondrous fortune came to me solely because I remained the true servant of the great Āḻvār who incarnated in the generous land of Thirukkuṟugūr.

## purport

By experiencing the pāsurams of Nammāḻvār and witnessing their profound effects, Madhurakavi Āḻvār comes to appreciate the true greatness of his master. It is solely through the grace of Nammāḻvār that he is able to experience Bhagavān Himself. Without the intervention of the Ācārya, the greatness of Bhagavān would have remained inaccessible to him. Therefore, every spiritual experience, every insight, and every glimpse of the Divine is attributed entirely to the compassion and blessings of Nammāḻvār.

Seeing the unparalleled devotion of Madhurakavi Āḻvār towards his Ācārya, Nammāḻvār desired that Bhagavān should reveal Himself to his disciple. Bhagavān, who never allows the wishes of His devotees and Ācāryas to go unfulfilled, gladly accepted this desire. Just as a loving child listens to the words of its mother, Bhagavān listened to Nammāḻvār and manifested Himself before Madhurakavi Āḻvār. Delighted by the extraordinary *Ācārya Śeṣatvam* displayed by Madhurakavi Āḻvār, Bhagavān revealed Himself in His enchanting form as Nīlamegha Śyāma, the dark cloud-hued Lord whose beauty captivates all beings.

Yet, even after receiving this divine vision, Madhurakavi Āḻvār does not attribute it to his own merit. He recognizes that this blessing has come solely through the grace, desire, and intercession of Nammāḻvār. Therefore, while he offers his reverence to Deivapirān, the Lord of the Nityasūris, he does so with the understanding that Bhagavān became accessible to him only because of his Ācārya's compassion. The vision of Bhagavān becomes yet another testimony to the greatness of Nammāḻvār.

Nammāḻvār's heart is filled with immeasurable love for Bhagavān and for Paramapada, the eternal abode where the Nityasūris render uninterrupted service to the Lord. Endowed with countless auspicious qualities and completely absorbed in devotion to Bhagavān, Nammāḻvār stands as the ideal Ācārya. For Madhurakavi Āḻvār, such a master alone is worthy of complete surrender. It is because he surrendered to Nammāḻvār that Bhagavān chose to reveal Himself to him.

Madhurakavi Āḻvār therefore proclaims that he has no desire to approach Bhagavān independently. To seek Bhagavān without the guidance and grace of an Ācārya would be, in his eyes, a grave mistake. The proper path is to approach the Lord through the lineage of Ācāryas, beginning with Nammāḻvār. Only through the mediation of the Ācārya does Bhagavān become truly known, loved, and attained. Thus, he does not adore Bhagavān separately; he adores Bhagavān because Bhagavān is beloved to Nammāḻvār. The Lord becomes precious to him because He is precious to his master.

For Madhurakavi Āḻvār, Nammāḻvār remains his refuge, his master, and his everything. The greatness of Bhagavān is not denied, but it is experienced entirely through the lens of Ācārya-bhakti. Even if, due to his own misfortune and accumulated karma, he were somehow to lose the intensity of his devotion towards Nammāḻvār, he declares that he would still remain devoted to Bhagavān, for such devotion itself is a gift received from his Ācārya. The bond established by Nammāḻvār can never truly be broken.
