## english_scripts

ēdamil taṇṇumai ekkam mattaḻi
yāz kuzal muzavamōḍiśai tiśai kezumi
kīdaṅgaḻ pāḍinar kinnarar keruḍargaḻ
kandaruvar avar kaṅguluḻ ellām
mādavar vānavar śāraṇar iyakkar
śittarum mayaṅginar tiruvaḍi ttozuvān
ādalil avarkku nāḻ ōlakkam aruḻa
araṅgattammā ! paḻḻi ezundaruḻāyē

## meaning

Oh Lord of Śrīraṅgam! Throughout the night, the semi-divine beings - Kinnaras, Garuḍas, and melodiously gifted Gandharvas - have been singing stotras eulogizing You. Some are playing on immaculate kettle-drums, vīṇā, ekkam (single-stringed instrument), diverse percussion and stringed instruments (yāḻ), and flutes. The nādam from this celestial symphony has enveloped all cardinal directions. These divine entities have assembled at Your sacred sannidhi, offering profound obeisance to Your divine lotus feet.

Accompanying them are the extraordinary saints of supreme austerities - celestial beings like Cāraṇas, Yakṣas, and Siddhas - who are trembling with ecstatic anticipation of Your divine darśanam. Some are even fainting from the overwhelming excitement of imminent spiritual revelation. Oh Lord of Śrīraṅgam! Bestow Your gracious sevai upon all these ardent devotees, bless us collectively, and awaken from Your yogic nidrā, rising from Your serpentine couch!

## purport

Alvar's feeling can put into as "Oh Lord of Śrīraṅgam! Throughout the night, the semi-divine beings - Kinnaras, Garuḍas, and melodiously gifted Gandharvas - have been singing stotras eulogizing You. Some are playing on immaculate kettle-drums, vīṇā, ekkam (single-stringed instrument), diverse percussion and stringed instruments (yāḻ), and flutes. The nādam from this celestial symphony has enveloped all cardinal directions. These divine entities have assembled at Your sacred sannidhi, offering profound obeisance to Your divine lotus feet. Accompanying them are the extraordinary saints of supreme austerities - celestial beings like Cāraṇas, Yakṣas, and Siddhas - who are trembling with ecstatic anticipation of Your divine darśanam. Some are even fainting from the overwhelming excitement of imminent spiritual revelation. Oh Lord of Śrīraṅgam! Bestow Your gracious sevai upon all these ardent devotees, bless us collectively, and awaken from Your yogic nidrā, rising from Your serpentine couch!"

Here Alvār takes the side of those devatās who are oozing bhakti, waiting for the glance of Rañganthar's divine form. While waiting for the doors to open, these celestial beings faint down from their devotion. Hence, Alvār tales their side requesting Rañganthar to wake up and shower grace.
