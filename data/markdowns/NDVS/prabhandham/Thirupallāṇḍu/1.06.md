## tamil_scripts

## english_scripts

endai tandai tandai tandai tam mūttappan
ēz paḍi kāl toḍaṅgi
vandu vazivazi āṭceyginṟōm
tiruvōṇa ttiruvizavil andiyam pōdil ari uruvāgi
ariyai azittavanai
pandanai tīra ppallāṇḍu
pallāyirattāṇḍenṟu pāḍudumē

## meaning

Our lineage has rendered uninterrupted service to the Lord across many generations. Let us now praise Him and sing, “May You live long for many thousands of years!” Let us glorify the Lord who removed all our limitations and who, in the majestic lion form, destroyed the tyrant Hiraṇyakaśipu at twilight.

## purport

Called by Periyāḻvār, the *prapannas* whose sole desire is to serve Bhagavān gather together and join in singing His glories. In unison, they proclaim that they were born for the very purpose of glorifying the Lord. Ordinarily, true devotees do not speak of themselves as great devotees, for such expressions may arise from pride. However, in this instance, the assembled *Bhāgavatas* speak in this manner only to reassure Periyāḻvār that they too stand alongside him in offering protection to Bhagavān. Their intention is not self-glorification, but the affirmation that they are dedicated solely to His welfare, generation after generation.

They declare that for seven generations their families have been singing the Lord's glories and offering auspicious benedictions for His protection. They seek neither *ātmānubhava* nor material prosperity. Their only aspiration is *bhagavad-kainkarya*. Therefore, they have gathered to sing for the protection of Bhagavān and to celebrate His divine qualities.

The devotees proclaim that they sing the glories of the Lord who appeared under the sacred *Śravaṇa* Nakṣatra. They praise the Lord who incarnated as Narasiṁha to destroy the tyrant Hiraṇyakaśipu and protect His devotee Prahlāda. From generation to generation, they have continued to sing the auspicious protection of that same Narasiṁha. They behold Him not merely as the fierce destroyer of evil, but as the beautiful Divine Lion whose appearance was motivated entirely by compassion for His devotee.

In their loving devotion, they imagine that after slaying Hiraṇyakaśipu and restoring righteousness, the Lord might have become fatigued from His tremendous effort. Filled with *vātsalya-bhāva*, they therefore offer their praises as a form of service and comfort. Just as Periyāḻvār blessed Bhagavān with *pallāṇḍu*, these devotees too join in the same spirit and exclaim:

“May that Narasiṁha live long for countless ages and eons! May the beautiful Lord who destroyed Hiraṇyakaśipu and protected Prahlāda ever flourish! May He be blessed with eternal auspiciousness!”

Thus, the chorus of devotees joins Periyāḻvār in singing *pallāṇḍu*, not for any personal gain, but solely out of boundless love and concern for their beloved Lord.
