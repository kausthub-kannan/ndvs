## bio

Vaḍakku Tiruvīḍhi Piḷḷai, also known as Kṛṣṇapādar, was a distinguished teacher in the Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāyam and a devoted disciple of Nampiḷḷai. He belonged to the lineage of Muḍumbai Nambi, one of the seventy-four _siṃhāsanādhipatis_ established by Śrī Rāmānuja. His father was Iḷaiyāḻvān, and he had a brother named Mannār. Vaḍakku Tiruvīḍhi Piḷḷai received the name Kṛṣṇapādar and came to be known as Vaḍakku Tiruvīḍhi Piḷḷai because he lived in the northern street of Śrīraṅgam.

He took refuge at the holy feet of Nampiḷḷai and studied the meanings of _Tiruvāymoḻi_ under him through the traditional _kālākṣepa_ method. With great dedication, he recorded the teachings imparted by his ācārya on palm leaves, preserving the explanations delivered during the daily discourses. After completing his records, he presented them as the _Īḍu muppattārāyirappaḍi_, a commentary comprising thirty-six thousand _granthams_. This work, also known as the _Īḍu vyākhyānam_, became one of the most important commentaries on Nammāḻvār’s _Tiruvāymoḻi_ and a major contribution to the preservation of the Śrīvaiṣṇava commentarial tradition.

Vaḍakku Tiruvīḍhi Piḷḷai was also the father of two eminent teachers, Piḷḷai Lokācārya and Aḻagiya Maṇavāḷa Perumāḷ Nāyaṉār, who made significant contributions to the tradition. Through his devotion to Nampiḷḷai and his careful preservation of the ācārya’s discourses, Vaḍakku Tiruvīḍhi Piḷḷai played a vital role in ensuring that the profound meanings of _Tiruvāymoḻi_ would be transmitted to future generations.

His legacy rests on his unwavering commitment to his ācārya’s teachings and his contribution to the _Īḍu_ commentary. By recording Nampiḷḷai’s discourses with care and devotion, he helped establish a lasting foundation for the study and transmission of _Bhagavad Viṣayam_ within the Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāyam.
