## bio

Periyavāccāṉ Piḷḷai was one of the most distinguished disciples of Nampiḷḷai and an eminent teacher in the Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāyam. He studied the traditional śāstras and the profound meanings of the _Divya Prabandham_ under Nampiḷḷai at Śrīraṅgam. Through his ācārya’s discourses on _Bhagavad Viṣayam_, he gained a deep understanding of Nammāḻvār’s _Tiruvāymoḻi_ and became renowned for his ability to preserve and explain the teachings of the tradition.

Nampiḷḷai entrusted Periyavāccāṉ Piḷḷai with the task of composing a commentary on _Tiruvāymoḻi_. Having learnt the meanings directly from his ācārya, he composed a commentary comprising twenty-four thousand _granthams_, comparable in its scope to the _Śrīmad Rāmāyaṇam_. This work became an important contribution to the preservation and transmission of the meanings of Nammāḻvār’s divine verses. His work reflects the traditional method of learning under an ācārya and conveying the received meanings faithfully to future generations.

Periyavāccāṉ Piḷḷai’s contribution extended beyond this commentary. As a close disciple of Nampiḷḷai, he belonged to the distinguished circle of scholars who received and transmitted the teachings of _Bhagavad Viṣayam_. His learning and service helped ensure that the profound meanings of the _Divya Prabandham_ continued to flourish within the Śrīvaiṣṇava tradition.

Periyavāccāṉ Piḷḷai is remembered for his devotion to Nampiḷḷai, his mastery of the _Divya Prabandham_, and his contribution to its commentarial tradition. By composing his commentary on _Tiruvāymoḻi_ in accordance with his ācārya’s instruction, he helped preserve the teachings of Nammāḻvār and strengthen the unbroken transmission of sampradāyic knowledge through the ācārya lineage.
