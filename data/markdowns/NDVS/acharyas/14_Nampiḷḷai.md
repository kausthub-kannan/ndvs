## bio

Nampiḷḷai was born in the agrahāram of Nambūr and received instruction in the traditional śāstras from Nañjīyar. He became the principal teacher who carried the Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāyam forward after his ācārya. Residing at Śrīraṅgam, Nampiḷḷai became renowned for his discourses on _Bhagavad Viṣayam_, the meanings of Nammāḻvār’s _Tiruvāymoḻi_. His learning, devotion, and ability to explain the profound meanings of the sampradāyam attracted a vast number of disciples, among whom were Periyavāccāṉ Piḷḷai, Vaḍakku Tiruvīthippiḷḷai, Piṉpaḻagiya Perumāḷ Jīyar, and Eyunni Madhava Perumāḷ. His gatherings were so large that a king, seeing his disciples emerge after a discourse, wondered whether they belonged to the sannidhi of Namperumāḷ or were the disciples of Nampiḷḷai.

Nampiḷḷai was known not only for his scholarship but also for his extraordinary humility and respect for fellow Śrīvaiṣṇavas. Once, Kaṇḍāḍai Toḻappar, jealous of Nampiḷḷai’s growing reputation, rebuked him harshly in the presence of Periya Perumāḷ. Nampiḷḷai bore the insult without retaliation and later went to Toḻappar’s house, seeking forgiveness for any offence he himself might have committed. Deeply moved by this humility, Toḻappar recognised his greatness and called him _Lokācāryar_, acknowledging that he was worthy of being the teacher of the entire world. Toḻappar himself then became a devoted disciple and learnt many important meanings of the śāstras from him.

Nampiḷḷai’s greatness was also evident in the devotion of his disciples. Piṉpaḻagiya Perumāḷ Jīyar, who served him closely, once fell seriously ill and wished to remain in this world longer to continue experiencing the Lord of Śrīraṅgam and serving his ācārya. Nampiḷḷai’s discourses inspired such deep attachment to the divine presence at Śrīraṅgam that his disciples regarded the opportunity to listen to _Bhagavad Viṣayam_ as an extraordinary blessing. On another occasion, during a dangerous crossing of the Kāverī, a devoted woman disciple risked her life to ensure Nampiḷḷai’s safety. When she later described how she had been saved in the river, the incident became an example of the profound faith that disciples placed in their ācārya.

Nampiḷḷai played a central role in preserving and transmitting the meanings of _Tiruvāymoḻi_. His discourses became the foundation for important commentaries composed by his disciples. Periyavāccāṉ Piḷḷai, following his ācārya’s instruction, composed a commentary on _Tiruvāymoḻi_ containing twenty-four thousand _granthams_. Vaḍakku Tiruvīthippiḷḷai recorded Nampiḷḷai’s discourses in the _Īḍu muppattārāyirappaḍi_, containing thirty-six thousand _granthams_. Through these works and the many scholars he trained, Nampiḷḷai ensured that the profound meanings of the _Divya Prabandham_ would continue to be studied and transmitted through the ācārya lineage.

Nampiḷḷai’s legacy rests on his mastery of _Bhagavad Viṣayam_, his humility in the face of insult, his affection for his disciples, and his dedication to preserving the teachings of his ācārya, Nañjīyar. By guiding eminent scholars and nurturing generations of Śrīvaiṣṇavas, he became one of the most influential teachers in the tradition and a central figure in the transmission of Nammāḻvār’s _Tiruvāymoḻi_.
