# Namāmyaham Drāviḍa Veda Sāgaram (NDVS)

---

> *bhaktāmṛtaṁ viśva-janānumodanaṁ*
> *sarvārtha-daṁ śrī-śaṭhakopa-vāṅmayam*
> *sahasra-śākhopaniṣat-samāgamaṁ*
> *namāmy ahaṁ drāviḍa-veda-sāgaram*

*"I worship the vast Ocean of the Drāviḍa Veda — the divine teachings of Śrī Śaṭhakopa (Nammāḻvār), the very essence of the countless Upaniṣads. It is nectar for the devotees of Bhagavān, delights all beings, and bestows every auspicious blessing."*

---

## About NDVS

**Namāmyaham Drāviḍa Veda Sāgaram (NDVS)** is a devotional initiative dedicated to preserving, studying, and sharing the timeless hymns of the twelve Āḻvārs. Their collective work, the **Nālāyira Divya Prabandham** — comprising 4,000 Tamil pāsurams — is revered in the Śrīvaiṣṇava sampradāya as the **Drāviḍa Veda**.

The Āḻvārs, regarded as incarnations of Bhagavān's eternal attendants (*Nitya Sūris*), appeared by divine will to illuminate the path of *bhakti* and *śaraṇāgati*. Through their divine *anubhavams*, they glorified Śrīman Nārāyaṇa, celebrated His auspicious qualities, and sanctified the 108 Divya Deśams — leaving behind a spiritual treasure that continues to inspire devotees across generations.

For over two years, NDVS has been devoted to building a comprehensive platform that brings the entire Divya Prabandham together in one well-organised and accessible place. The objective extends beyond simply presenting the pāsurams — it is to create a structured, searchable, and enriching resource for devotees, students, researchers, and seekers around the world.

## Content Structure

Each pāsuram in the NDVS platform is carefully organised with the following:

- **Original Tamil text** — the pāsuram as composed by the Āḻvār
- **English meaning** — a faithful rendering of the verse
- **Purport** — a condensed synthesis of various Pūrvācārya commentaries
- **Anubhavams** — the Āḻvār's devotional experience, including *śaraṇāgati*, *vātsalya*, *śṛṅgāra / nāyikā-bhāva*, and others
- **Associated Divya Deśams** — the sacred temples glorified in the verse
- **Related avatārams** — the divine forms of Bhagavān referenced
- **Contextual references and classifications**

NDVS also aims to provide powerful search capabilities, advanced filtering, cross-references, multilingual support, and an intuitive reading experience across desktop and mobile devices. English and Tamil will be supported initially, with additional languages planned in the future.

## Prabandham Mālai — Coming Soon
